## 👋 About me

I'm **Avinash Chandan**, a **senior backend engineer** who builds **production-grade AI agents, real-time voice AI, and distributed backend systems**. My work sits where large language models meet real business infrastructure: streaming speech-to-text, tool-calling LLMs, text-to-speech, durable workflows, and the integration gateways that tie them to enterprise software.

I'm happiest turning a messy, multi-step workflow into something that feels simple to the user, and keeping it reliable, observable, and secure underneath.

> **Curious by default.** I like understanding why a system behaves the way it does, then making it boringly dependable.
